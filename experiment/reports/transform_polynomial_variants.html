Why the raw polynomial bases differ from each other: they span the same space, so a trajectory has the same reconstruction in each, but the coefficients are different linear mixtures and the quantizer rounds each coefficient on its own. Equal-accuracy steps keep the rounding error per coefficient identical, so the comparison is fair. Bases with condition number above 1e+08 are not usable for tokenization at that size and are skipped.
