How much do the strongest coefficients move when a trajectory is edited?

Generated 2026-11-20 23:43 by scripts/transform_topk_shift_analysis.py. Synthetic shapes only; companion to the token-shift analysis and the fingerprint analysis.

Result (N = 64, top 2 coefficients = 3%). After an edit, the original top-3% coefficients move on average 29.2 bins with DCT-II (rank 5 of 18) and 30.0 bins with Haar (rank 3); largest: PCA (learned) (32.7). Relative to their own size that is 22% for DCT and 20% for Haar. 16% (DCT) and 16% (Haar) of the top-3% positions are replaced by other coefficients.

Method

400 random smooth shapes (cosine + cubic + Gaussian bump + tanh), the 9 visible edits and the 18 transforms of the token-shift analysis, equal-accuracy quantizer step 0.02 on a range of 2 (so one bin means the same reconstruction error in every transform). For each shape the top 3% coefficients by |bin| are taken (k = ceil(0.03 N): N = 32: 1, N = 64: 2, N = 128: 4), the edit is applied, and the coefficients are quantized again.

  • Bin shift: mean |bin change| of the original top-k coefficients (same positions before and after).
  • Relative shift: bin shift divided by those coefficients' original |bin|.
  • Set change: share of the original top-k positions that drop out of the edited shape's top-k.
  • Rank shift: top-k positions sorted by strength before and after, paired by rank, mean |index difference|. For wavelets an index distance mixes scale and time, so this one is only comparable within a family.
